i've been working remotely for 5 years. it's nice actually. i don't have to spend a lot of money and time to commute. i just wake up and few minutes later i'm already on my laptop working. when i finish, it only takes me a few steps to finally lay on the bed. it is such a privilege to keep working from home when most companies have obligated their employees to go back to the office whether it is fully or hybrid.

however, in this moment i want to talk about the cost of working remotely, which is loneliness. i'm actually introvert, i don't talk too much, i mostly just listen when i hangout with friends, so being alone is not something unfamiliar. 

but then after an extensive period of being alone, it starts to "torment" me. i miss interacting with a lot of people. if i can't for several reasons such as the fact that i work from home and there are days when i don't have a lot of meetings, at least i want to be able to hear people chatting. it brings peace to my mind. it distracts me from thinking too much especially about the future and its possible worst scenario.

yeah honestly i've been thinking about bad thing that may happen in my career in the future. one of the reason is ai. i'm not saying it is bad. it is good actually, it helps a lot for my day to day work. however, it brings some serious issues:

- i start to lose spark of joy at my work. before, i can fully focus on working   on a task in one, two, or even three weeks, then fixing bugs on the next couple of days, then a new sprint came. new sprint means new work. i enjoy this kind of work settings. 

it started to feel different nowadays. i can finish my tasks faster, but then i don't really know what to do next. there's no longer project manager that can assign me new tickets when my plate is empty. i have to look for tasks myself and it really makes me uncomfortable when i couldn't find one which often leads to frustration and loss of motivation. other than that, my team no longer have QA as well. those guys were very reliable in catching edge cases that i couldn't even think of during development, but now i have to find it myself. well, ai can help me though, but it still feels different as there is less human interaction

- i see a lot of people showcasing their advance use of ai, how they build products quickly and used by at least hundreds of people on their own to make money, or how rapid ai development is. every week there are new tools, new models, new this, new that. it's overwhelming, a lot more than how fast web framework evolved. it makes me unsure which one to focus on and undermines my confidence, makes me wonder if i can keep up as i feel like i haven't been as advanced as the others. i'm still a simple man using claude code cli and vscode extension with not so many skills and subagents. and no, i'm not running multi agents at the same time for different projects, my laptop can't keep up

- i'm mostly a frontend engineer though i also touch some backend which makes me somewhat consider myself as fullstack. i feel like frontend is coming to an end. not because it is obsolete so people leave it, but it becomes less and less valuable. people who have no frontend development background can generate performant frontend apps that work. designers in my team, for example. as long as you can design or have good reference, ai can help build the frontend part in minutes.

besides that, i would say frontend is relatively easy compared to backend because it doesn't involve complex system design, infra, or security. well it still involves, but not as complex as how i see it in backend. it is worrying me as a frontend engineer. if i can't improve my knowledge outside of frontend, my value in tech keeps eroding which leads to hard to find a job with increased salary and it will annoy me if i can't give more to my family

these thoughts keep occupying my head especially when i'm alone. i start to feel tortured with everything going around inside my head. however somehow, when i meet people and chat with them, i feel calmer, i enjoy every interaction, i can even talk about the complicated things in my head to them while laughing. it feels like the problem i have is lighter when i'm around people

so these days, if there's a schedule to meet people, i can't wait for the day to come. be it joining local tech community meetup, working from office on the last friday of each month, working from cafe with my college friends, going to my mother's house on weekends, going to my parent in laws in different town once a month, or even going to psychologist. yes i gotta admit, i need help from professional in mental health to survive

it feels really good to meet people even as introvert because i'm a human after all and human is a social being. even if i can work on some complex projects by myself with help from ai agents, i still prefer being on a team because i can build real connection, share accountability, or seek opinion from people with real life experience

i'm grateful that i decide to stay close to my family after marriage because i can meet them even though just during weekend. it always feels like home when i come back to my mother's house and it's nice to see them doing alright directly. with this, i can also still come to my office every month or meet my college friends once in a while whose group chat is still quite active because most of them still live nearby. it would be tougher to build connection with new people especially when i'm really in need for human interactions

and finally, i'm now open to opportunity for working hybrid or wfo as it increases the probability of me meeting like minded people.
